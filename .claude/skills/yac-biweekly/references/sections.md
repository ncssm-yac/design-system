# Standing sections

The biweekly has a familiar shape. Use this list to propose a running order, then to ask what is missing. "Always" sections are in every deck. Others run when the agenda has them.

Templates are in `slides/templates/`. Minutes are typical, not fixed; the whole meeting is 60 minutes with 5–10 of buffer.

| # | Section | When | Typical owner | Min | Template(s) |
|:-|:-|:-|:-|:-|:-|
| 1 | Cover + opener | Always | Seven or the exec | 5 | `cover` (title, date, time and the opener prompt on one slide; it stays up while people join) |
| 2 | Teammate Tribute | Always | Teammate Tribute lead (Kaavya) | 3 | `teammate-tribute` |
| 3 | Shape of tonight | Always | Facilitator | 1 | `agenda` (4–6 groups, no times) |
| 4 | Announcements | Always | Facilitator, with others for their items | 3–5 | `announcements` for 2–3 short items, `event` for an event with a date, place and RSVP |
| 5 | School and Foundation updates | When there is news | Akhil or the exec | 5–15 | `divider` + `statement`, `two-column`, `stat-chart` |
| 6 | Special assignments | When active | The assignment owner | 3–5 each | `divider` once, then one `statement` or an INSERT slot per owner |
| 7 | Committee and project updates | When on the agenda | Committee chairs | 3 each | `statement`, `two-column` |
| 8 | Social media update and ask | Always | Digital Community (Lily) | 3 | `social-update` |
| 9 | YAC Families | When scheduled | Families lead (Lily) | 5–10 | `statement` (the mission), `breakout` |
| 10 | Breakouts and shareout | When on the agenda | The breakout lead | 5–10 | `breakout`, then `statement` for "Back together" if rooms report |
| 11 | Akhil's Adage | When running | Akhil | 3 | three `statement` slides: the question, a case, the adage |
| 12 | Event recap | After a big event | Event owner | 3 | `stat-chart` for attendance, `photo-grid` |
| 13 | The Catalyst Award | Always | Awards lead (this cycle: check the Leadership minutes) | 4 | `catalyst-tease` then `catalyst-reveal`, always a pair |
| 14 | Before we go | Always, always last | Facilitator | 3 | `closing`, with a **Next biweekly** card |

Order: the usual running order is 1, 2, 3, 4, then updates (5–7), social media (8), culture (9–11), recap (12), the Catalyst (13), Before we go (14). The Leadership minutes' "Proposed Biweekly Agenda" overrides this when it differs (for example, Teammate Tribute "right at the beginning, after warmup").

## Prompts for the exec

Ask only what the sources left open. Offer a default whenever the sources suggest one.

### Cover + opener
- Who runs the opener, and what is the prompt? (Past ones: a chat question such as "What's an aura hit you've taken so far this month?")
- Is the opener a game that needs its own slide?

### Teammate Tribute
- Who is being celebrated this time, and who presents? (Often decided at Tuesday leadership.)
- Where does it go in the order, early or later?
- Are the teammate quotes in, and from where? Are they written yet, or should the slide be a placeholder?
- Anything the lead wants kept vague on the slide?

### Announcements
Walk this list and ask which apply. Group what is left into 2–5 blocks, one label plus one line each.
- **School news.** Anything from NCSSM leadership, Morganton, admissions, trustees or the Board of Governors, new programs or policies?
- **Foundation news.** Alumni Weekend, receptions, the giving challenge, new Foundation staff or tools, anything from the development committee?
- **Upcoming events.** YAC events (pickleball, family outings, spring socials, campus events), NCSSM or Foundation events, workshops. For each: date, time, place, who it is for, RSVP link (becomes a QR code), who speaks to it.
- **Deadlines and asks.** Forms due, nominations, RSVPs, anything members must do this week.
- **Member housekeeping.** Provisional check-ins, OKR mid-semester scores, attendance, calendar invite acceptance, reminders about meeting norms.
- **Tech updates.** Yakkety, Yaktion Items, YAC Busy Sync, the engagement app, the database.
- **Time or schedule changes.** Meeting times, leadership meeting changes, anything moving.
- **Shout-outs.** Wins worth naming that are not the Catalyst.

### School and Foundation updates
- Is there news that needs more than an announcement line? Who presents it?
- Numbers or dates on slides: where do they come from, and are they confirmed?
- Is there a question to put to the room?

### Special assignments and committee updates
- Which special assignments report this time? Last meeting's promises (for example, "full proposal next biweekly", "rollout at the next meeting") are due now unless the exec says otherwise.
- Does the owner bring their own slides (INSERT slot) or talk over one slide of ours?
- Is anyone asking the room for something (volunteers, nominations, input)?

### Social media update and ask
- Where is posting right now (schedule, latest post, anything coming)?
- What is the ask this time (like and reshare, submit content, fill the form)?
- Is there a screenshot or reel still for the slide, and who sends it?

### YAC Families
- Is there a family activity or gathering this month? Budget and theme?
- Breakouts: which rooms, how long, who reports back, or do notes go to YAC General Chat?
- Room lists come from the YAC Families doc; confirm if they changed.

### Akhil's Adage
- Is one running tonight? If so, the question, the case and the adage.

### Event recap
- Which event, and what is the confirmed attendance? Who sends photos?

### The Catalyst Award
- Who is the recipient and who announces it? (Often decided at Tuesday leadership.)
- A warm, specific tagline and a one-line why. If it is not decided, the reveal stays a placeholder.
- Is there a headshot (the YAC headshots folder) or a retreat photo?
- Tagline style: clever, warm, specific to what the person did, never backhanded. Examples from past cycles: "The Beast with an Excel Sheet", "Never Missed a Shot", "Spreading the Vibes", "Prototyper Extraordinaire". Offer one pick and at most two alternates, and mark it "swap if you like".

### Before we go
- What do members need to remember or do before the next meeting? (Dates, RSVPs, homework, family plans.)
- Is there a link people should scan?
- Confirm the **next biweekly** date and time from the calendar.

## Wrap-up questions (ask once, near the end)
- Is there anything sensitive to keep vague or off slides?
- Is any segment bringing its own slides or a video? (INSERT slot or divider only.)
- Which photos are still needed, and who sends them?
- Anything members raised since the minutes that should be on the agenda?
