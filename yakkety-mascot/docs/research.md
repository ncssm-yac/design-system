# Research: AI assistant avatars and mascots

This research was gathered in October 2026 to choose Yakkety's look. The claims summarise the sources linked below. Most are trade press and opinion, not studies, unless noted.

## 1. What AI assistants and agents use

- **Abstract symbols dominate.** Gemini's spark stands for "illumination". Perplexity's asterisk reads as both a spark and an open book. OpenAI's identity centres on "the point", a black circle. ([Ebaq Design](https://www.ebaqdesign.com/blog/ai-company-branding))
- **Everything looks the same.** Designers say AI marks have converged on sparkles, swirls and gradients, so you have to read the name to tell them apart. Fast Company calls the gradient the defining colour scheme of the AI era. ([Creative Bloq](https://creativebloq.com/design/logos-icons/why-do-all-ai-company-logos-look-the-same), [braindetox](https://braindetox.kr/en/posts/ai_logo_design_convergence_2026.html))
- **"AI employee" products use plain marks.** Lindy, an AI executive assistant, uses a wordmark-style logo with no character. ([Toolradar](https://toolradar.com/logo/lindy-ai))
- **Agent frameworks use team imagery.** CrewAI's logo is two people rowing a boat. ([PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/crewai/0.75.0))
- **Chief of staff agents** have no visual convention yet; the category is new. ([First AI Movers](https://www.firstaimovers.com/p/personal-ai-chief-of-staff-2026-always-on-executive-agents))

**Takeaway:** avoid the spark, swirl and gradient orb. A character stands out.

## 2. The 2026 wave of cute agent mascots

- **OpenAI Dots** (DevDay, 29 September 2026): always-on agents with "cutesy, blobby" characters (a triangle with a bow tie, a bunny with headphones, a cloud with a beret). Users can name a Dot and pick its avatar. The goal is for the agent to feel like a "partner to work with" rather than a chatbot window. OpenAI plans "teams of Dots working together." ([Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91615068/openai-dots-agent-crew-of-friendly-mascots), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/openai-launches-dots-its-bubbly-agentic-avatar/), [Design Compass](https://designcompass.org/en/2026/09/30/openai-dots-always-on-ai-agents/))
- **Meta Muse / Jolly**: a cream, fuzzy, egg-shaped character with beady eyes and pink cheeks, designed by Muse product design lead Alex Cornell. ([Axios](https://axios.com/2026/09/25/ai-doom-meta-muse-mascot))
- **The backlash against Jolly:**
  - Muse is 18+, but Fairplay says Jolly is "designed in ways that strongly attract young children" and compared it to a Teletubby.
  - Critics named the technique *Kindchenschema*: using baby features to win trust.
  - Jolly became a meme, including suggestive memes.
  - Sources: [Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/ai/metas-new-ai-avatar-is-making-me-feel-infantilised), [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/software/ai/i-want-to-punt-metas-muse-ai-mascot-back-into-the-cursed-void-from-whence-it-came/), [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/metas-muse-ships-an-18-ai-agent-behind-a-kids-toy-mascot), [AV Club](https://www.avclub.com/jolly-ai-agent-subject-of-sexy-memes-meta), [SAN](https://san.com/cc/metas-ai-agent-is-very-cute-will-that-distract-from-its-security-risks/)
- **Why everyone is doing it:** agents read your mail, move your meetings and sometimes spend money. A friendly character makes that access easier to grant, which critics read as "harder to say no to". ([Il Foglio](https://www.ilfoglio.it/en/tech/2026/10/03/news/the-future-is-made-up-of-cute-emojis--409470), [Il Post](https://www.ilpost.it/2026/10/03/ai-pucciose-meta-openai/))
- **Microsoft Mico** (Copilot, October 2025): a blob-shaped face "defined not by fixed features, but by behavior". It changes colour as you talk to it, and it's optional and easy to turn off, unlike Clippy. ([Psyop](https://psyop.com/work/microsoft-ai-mico), [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/microsoft/meet-the-new-clippy-microsoft-unveils-copilots-mico-avatar))

## 3. Lessons from Clippy and from trust research

- Clippy failed because it interrupted, repeated itself, stared, and never learned what users preferred. Having a face wasn't the problem. ([The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/humanity-vs-clippy-lessons-from-microsofts-failed-virtual-assistant/))
- A meta-analysis of 62 studies found a moderate positive link between human-like features and trust. A 2025 study found that warmth, not apparent competence, is what drives it. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2202.01077), [GI](https://dl.gi.de/items/6d29b2c1-1669-499a-95e6-88f39533ff8f/full))

## 4. Lessons from Duolingo's Duo

- The mascot *is* the brand, not a sidekick.
- Build an emotional range: the old owl only had happy and crying states.
- Write usage guidelines for use outside the app early.
- Watch colour: Duo's green "vibrates" on some backgrounds.
- Plan for a cast of characters, not just one.
- Sources: [Johnson Banks](https://www.johnsonbanks.co.uk/templates/work/duolingo), [Creative Review](https://www.creativereview.co.uk/duolingo-rebrand-johnson-banks/), [Duolingo design](https://design.duolingo.com/marketing/assets), [Olson Zaltman](https://olsonzaltman.com/devilish-duo-why-brand-mascots-still-have-a-place/)

## What we applied to Yakkety

1. Charming, not childish: confident eyes, no baby proportions. YAC users are students, so the "kids' toy" risk is real.
2. A distinctive silhouette (horns, fringe, wide head) so the character reads at 16px.
3. Five expressions built from the same parts.
4. One signature colour (Toffee), with no AI gradient.
5. A full-body icon so the mascot and the app icon always match.
6. Personality in the art and the copy, never in interruptions.
7. A simple silhouette that is hard to misuse in memes.
8. Room for a cast later: if Yakkety orchestrates sub-agents, smaller yaks could stand for them.
