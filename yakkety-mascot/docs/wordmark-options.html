Worth knowing
  • On a dark background, C's chocolate Y almost disappears. If you pick C, the dark-background version would swap the chocolate for bone, the way the horns' tips already are.
  • At 20px, C's dark Y separates from the rest of the word, and D's tips disappear. If you pick C or D, E becomes the small-size version (below about 32px tall), the way many logos have a simpler mark for small spaces.
  • Toffee on white is fine for a logo, but too light for small text like a footer. Each final version also gets a chocolate one-colour variant for small or single-colour use.
  • Once you pick, I'll add the wordmark, the lockups and their rules to the design system as SVG and PNG.
