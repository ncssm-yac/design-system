Mascot concept · v1

Meet Yakkety.

The YAC's AI secretary, drawn in a Duo-style language: flat, chunky shapes, huge eyes, no outlines, one bold colour. Built from simple geometric shapes so it holds up from a billboard down to a 16px favicon.

A round, shaggy yak whose fringe flops over big confident eyes, with horns that curl up like quotation marks for all that chatter.

Signature colour

App icon

The whole yak, framed for the icon

The icon is the full mascot, hooves and all, with room on every side, so it matches the character exactly. At 16px the horns, fringe and eyes still read as Yakkety.

Expressions

Five states, same parts

Following Duo's lesson: build an emotional range from day one. Every state changes only the eyes, mouth and one small extra, so the character stays consistent.

Colourways

One colour has to win

Duo owns green. Yakkety needs its own colour, and the brand works best if we commit to one. Toffee is my pick: warm, true to a real yak's coat, and far from every AI gradient.

Ground rules

What we borrowed, what stays ours

Borrowed from Duo's playbook

  • Flat colour, no outlines, no gradients
  • Built from circles and rounded shapes only
  • Eyes take up about half the face and carry the emotion
  • One signature colour plus a few tints
  • Full expression set, with the mascot as the brand

Kept distinct from Duo

  • No green; toffee is the signature colour
  • Fringe over the eyes instead of Duo's eye shapes
  • Horns, ears and a shaggy hem give a different silhouette
  • Freckled cheeks and a wide, friendly smile
  • Fun and friendly, but not a baby face (avoids the Jolly backlash)
